## AffectationScopeService & Scope Endpoints
- **Service**: `AffectationScopeService` in `esprit_D2F-competence/src/main/java/tn/esprit/d2f/competence/service/AffectationScopeService.java` encapsulates the permission logic for assigning competencies. It evaluates the current user's role (ADMIN, CUP, CHEF_DEPARTEMENT) against the target teacher’s UP and department.
- **Port**: `EnseignantScopePort` (JDBC repository) in `esprit_D2F-competence/src/main/java/tn/esprit/d2f/competence/repository/EnseignantScopePort.java` provides read‑only visibility of `formation.enseignants` for the current tenant.
- **Permissions**:
  * Grant `USAGE` on the `formation` schema and `SELECT` on `id`, `up_id`, `dept_id`, `mail`, `user_id` in `app_user_competence`.
  * `AuthorizationMatrix` grants `AFFECTATION_WRITE` to ADMIN, CUP and CHEF_DEPARTEMENT.
  * `AFFECTATION_READ` is granted to ADMIN, CUP and CHEF_DEPARTEMENT.
- **Endpoint**: `GET /api/v1/enseignant-competences/perimetre` returns a `PerimetreAffectationDTO` describing the current user's effective perimeter (set of UPs and/or departments). The front‑end uses this DTO to filter the list of teachers for assignment operations.
- **Front‑side**: The `AffectationPerimetre.ts` contract in `esprit_D2F-webapp/src/pages/competence/dto/AffectationPerimetre.ts` mirrors the DTO and is consumed by the component `EnseignantCompetenceController`. The visibility of teachers is now governed solely by the server‑side token claims (`sub`, `role`, `email`). Roles and scopes are attached to the token via the gateway `AuthorizationFilter`.
- **AGENTS.md** update ensures stakeholders understand the new scope service and the associated grants and endpoint, providing a clear trace of how tenant‑level permissions are enforced across the backend and front‑end layers.
